Our Story

One firm, built for demanding client profiles.

Growth Finance Partners is a Colorado-based financial advisory, fractional CFO, and accounting firm. We work directly with founders, executives, and investors — owning financial reporting, systems, accuracy, and communication while acting as a trusted extension of your leadership team.

Our experience spans multiple industries and business models. We bridge the gap when businesses outgrow bookkeeping but aren't yet ready for a full-time CFO. By combining deep expertise in commercial accounting, government contracting, privately held businesses, and executive leadership, we deliver the financial discipline, reporting, and strategic insights that leadership relies on when the stakes are high.

We typically partner with companies between $2M and $50M in annual revenue that need sophisticated financial leadership without the cost of a full-time CFO.
